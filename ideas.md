# AI Creative Studio Website Direction

## Theme options

### Theme 1: Editorial Signal
A premium creative-studio site with warm paper tones, deep plum typography, cobalt structure, and acid-lime moments. It feels authored, cultural, and campaign-ready rather than like a software dashboard.

Probability: 0.06

### Theme 2: Neon Systems Lab
A dark interface-inspired direction with electric cyan, violet, and magenta, framing the work as a fast, technical AI production system.

Probability: 0.04

### Theme 3: Soft Product Cinema
A calm, image-first direction built around ivory, soft black, and muted mineral colors, letting the example videos and product imagery carry the personality.

Probability: 0.08

## Chosen approach: Editorial Signal

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial art direction blended with independent creative-agency portfolio design.

### Core Principles
1. **Make the work the proof.** The video examples are the primary content, not decorative thumbnails.
2. **Use asymmetry with intention.** Offset columns, split compositions, and strong vertical rhythm should guide the eye through the offer.
3. **Balance warmth and edge.** Cream and plum establish trust; cobalt, coral, and acid lime create energy without looking like a generic AI dashboard.
4. **Keep the sales path obvious.** Every section should move from capability to proof to invitation.

### Color Philosophy
Cream creates approachability and gives media room to breathe. Deep plum adds confidence and authorship. Cobalt acts as the structural brand color, while acid lime and coral are used sparingly as signals for motion, action, or emphasis.

### Layout Paradigm
A scrolling editorial case-study layout: a split hero, a horizontal capability band, staggered video proof sections, a process rail, and a closing invitation. Avoid generic centered hero layouts and repetitive card grids.

### Signature Elements
- Vertical editorial labels and oversized serif headlines.
- Cobalt rules and acid-lime highlight bars used as navigation cues.
- Video frames treated like selected work in a creative director’s contact sheet.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like browsing a curated studio reel: videos play in place, buttons feel direct and tactile, and navigation anchors make the site easy to scan from an Instagram link.

### Animation
Use subtle reveal transitions on section entry, hover lift on video frames, and gentle color shifts on CTA buttons. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep transitions below 300ms.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for headlines and **Manrope** for body copy, labels, navigation, and buttons. Headlines should be oversized and editorial; body copy should remain compact, calm, and highly readable.

### Brand Essence
AI-assisted creative production for brands that need more usable content, more control, and more ways to test. Personality: **sharp, generous, inventive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and confident. CTAs are personal and low-pressure. Microcopy explains what the viewer should notice rather than making inflated claims.

Example lines:
- “One product brief. Many ways to make it move.”
- “Bring the product. We’ll bring the directions.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact monogram made from two offset brackets suggesting a frame, a prompt, and a play button. The mark should appear as a small cobalt-and-lime symbol beside the text wordmark “STUDIO / 01”.

### Signature Brand Color
**Cobalt Signal — #2E4FFF.** It is confident enough for B2B outreach, vivid enough to feel current, and distinctive against cream and plum.

## Style Decisions
- The website is a video-led outbound page, not a generic agency portfolio.
- Videos should remain playable in the page and be labeled by the capability they demonstrate.
- Avoid fabricated client names, testimonials, reviews, performance guarantees, or unsupported CAC claims.
- Use the uploaded examples as work samples and describe them accurately.
